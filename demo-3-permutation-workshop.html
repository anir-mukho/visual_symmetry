The 15-puzzle, and Loyd’s prize

In 1880 Sam Loyd offered $1000 to anyone who could swap the 14 and the 15 and slide the board back to order. He never paid out. Press his position below, then try. The quantity on the right never budges, whatever you do.

The invariant after each move

Every slide swaps the blank with a neighbour, so the sign flips — and the blank moves one step, so its distance home flips parity too. The product of the two cannot change. That is the whole proof.
